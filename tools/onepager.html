ups‑adaptor

Your UPS on the network. No PC required.
A USB‑only UPS is invisible to everything on your network. It needs a computer plugged into it, running all the time, just to say “the power is out”.

ups‑adaptor is that computer, shrunk to 36 × 58 mm. It speaks NUT on TCP 3493 — the protocol Home Assistant, Synology, TrueNAS, unraid and upsmon already understand — so nothing needs a plugin, a bridge or a driver.

Plug the UPS into the USB‑A port, power the board from USB‑C, and it appears on your network as ups‑adaptor.local.
GENERIC BY DESIGN

Not built for one model

Parses the USB HID Power Device descriptor at runtime rather than hard‑coding a UPS. Whatever your UPS reports, it forwards. Validated on a real APC Back‑UPS RS 1000G: 86 fields decoded into 20 NUT variables, no vendor quirk needed.

RECOVERS ITSELF

Power‑cycles a wedged UPS

VBUS to the UPS runs through a current‑limited load switch on a GPIO. When a UPS interface locks up — and they do — firmware cycles its power instead of someone walking to the closet.

RECONNECTS

Survives the network

Wi‑Fi drops are handled with a non‑blocking retry, not a watchdog reboot. The board also survives the UPS being unplugged, replugged and power‑cycled mid‑session without a reset.
