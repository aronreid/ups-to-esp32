Address

Automatic (DHCP) lets your router choose. A fixed address never changes, which is what a NAS or Home Assistant pointed at this board's IP needs. If the router does not answer from the fixed address, the board goes back to automatic until it restarts, so a typo cannot lose it.

Tailscale

Puts this board on your tailnet, so NUT and this page answer on its tailnet address from anywhere on it. Make an auth key in the Tailscale admin console under Settings, Keys. The board keeps it and never shows it again.

Log in

This board needs its username and password. Forgot them? Plug a computer into its USB-C port, open the serial console at 115200 and type clear-login: that removes only the login. Or press its BOOT button five times within four seconds, which clears its Wi-Fi too and starts it over in setup.
