# Contributing

Thank you for helping. Bug reports, UPS captures and test results are welcome
from anyone, with no paperwork: open an issue.

2. It is licensed under the licence of the files it changes:

   design files.
3. You also grant Aron Reid a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free,
   irrevocable licence to use, modify and distribute your contribution under
   other terms as well, including commercial licences. This lets the project
   offer a commercial hardware licence and keep its licensing simple. You keep
   your copyright, and you may use your contribution however you like.

Mark this in each commit with a sign-off line, `git commit -s`:

    Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>

A pull request without it cannot be merged.
